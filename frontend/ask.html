BloggerBear operator assistant

The assistant is not available here.

A spoken briefing on the site's pipeline, for its operator. Sign in to ask what needs attention.

Things you can ask:

  • What needs my attention?
  • What are your prior suggestions?
  • I'm new, where should I start?
  • What can you do?
  • How does the project work?

    Tap the button, ask your question, and it stops when you stop speaking (or tap again). You can also hold it (or Space or Enter) while you speak: it keeps listening through your pauses until you let go, for up to a minute. "Test voice" checks the microphone and the spoken answers.

    Speech recognition does not work in this browser, so type your question instead. Chrome and Edge can listen.

    This browser has no speech recognition, so type your question instead.

    You can also ask how to do something with the Admin CLI ("how do I change how often topics run?") or to list your topics. The command is shown for you to copy; nothing is run from here.
